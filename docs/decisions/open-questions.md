# Open product questions

These do not block P0 coding. Safe defaults are in place; each needs a product answer before the related milestone ships.

| # | Question | Safe default in P0 | Needed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can one user belong to several companies, and how do they switch? | **Release 1 default (P1.6):** one company per account. An account that already belongs to a company cannot accept another invitation (clear message, audited). A removed member can be invited back or join another company. Supporting several companies later means adding a company switcher to tenant resolution; data is already keyed by company. | Product owner to confirm |
| 2 | Invitation lifecycle: who can invite, expiry, re-invite, deprovision behavior | **Release 1 default (P1.6):** company admins invite, change roles and remove members (`Collaboration:MinimumRoleToInvite`, `MinimumRoleToManageMembers`). Invitations last 7 days (`InvitationExpiryDays`), work once, are bound to the invited email and need a confirmed address (`RequireConfirmedEmailToAcceptInvitation`). Re-inviting an address replaces the earlier invitation. Removal takes effect on the next request, keeps past work attributed, cancels the person's pending reviews, and never removes the last company admin. | Product owner to confirm |
| 3 | Final proposal lifecycle terminology (§7.3) | **Release 1 default (P1.4):** states Draft → In progress → In review → Approved → Ready for submission → Submitted (outside GovBid Pro) → Won/Lost, plus Cancelled. Display names are configuration (`Proposals:StageLabels`, e.g. `"InReview": "Pink team"`); transitions stay fixed in code. Approved and later stages need TeamManager+ (`Proposals:MinimumRoleForFinalStages`); owners cannot self-review (`Proposals:AllowOwnerSelfReview`); mandatory items must be resolved before Ready for submission (`Proposals:RequireMandatoryRequirementsResolved`). | Product owner to confirm names |
| 4 | Which non-federal procurement sources to support | Adapter interface only; no other sources. | P2 |
| 5 | Notification frequency options and channels | **Release 1 default (P1.6):** saved searches alert `None`, `Daily` or `Weekly` (periods in `Collaboration:Alerts`), reporting each notice once per search. Channels: in-app always; email when a provider is configured, with a per-member opt-out. Assignments, review requests and decisions, tasks, comments and joins notify the people involved. No SMS or chat integrations. | Product owner to confirm |
| 6 | Response-type interpretation rules beyond explicit wording | Mapping rule v1 only maps explicit "RFI/RFQ/RFP" wording; everything else is `Undetermined` for a user to decide. | P1.1 |
| 7 | Malware scanning product and cost | **Decided (ADR-0005):** on Azure, Defender for Storage on-upload scanning (about US$10 per storage account per month plus US$0.15 per GB scanned, capped monthly), enabled by the Bicep templates. ClamAV stays the portable option for any other host. Downloads stay denied without a clean verdict. | Resolved 2026-11-18 |
| 8 | Email provider for confirmations and alerts | **Release 1 default (P1.6):** provider-neutral SMTP adapter behind `IEmailSender`; off until configured. Which service to use is still open. | Before production |
